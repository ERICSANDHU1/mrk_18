## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide directly
- **Account data:** name, email address, phone number, password, company/startup name, role.
- **Waitlist and Founding 500 application data:** responses you submit in application or waitlist forms, including details about your business, marketing goals, budget ranges, and challenges.
- **Business and marketing context:** information you share about your company, products, customers, positioning, goals, and strategy — via text or voice — so that mrk18 can give context-aware advice.
- **Voice input:** audio you speak to the web app or the Device, and the transcripts generated from it.
- **Payment data:** processed by our payment partners (e.g., Razorpay/Stripe or equivalent). We do not store full card numbers, CVV, or banking passwords on our servers. We retain transaction identifiers, plan type, billing name, and invoicing details.
- **Communications:** emails, support requests, feedback, and testimonials you send us.

### 2.2 Information collected when you connect marketing accounts
If you connect ad accounts, analytics, or marketing platforms (e.g., Meta Ads, Google Ads, analytics tools), we collect, with your authorization:
- Campaign structure, spend, performance metrics (impressions, clicks, CAC, conversions, ROAS and similar), audience configuration, and creative metadata.
- We access this data **read-first**: execution actions (launching or editing campaigns) occur only on your instruction and within your plan limits.
- You can disconnect any linked account at any time from your settings; we stop collecting new data from that source immediately.

### 2.3 Information collected by the Device
The mrk18 Device includes microphones and (in later versions) a camera. Because of this, we apply heightened safeguards:

- **Listening modes.** The Device supports a passive listening mode (wake-on-speech) and an active assistant mode. You control when the Device is on, and hardware/software indicators show when audio is being captured.
- **Audio and transcripts.** Captured audio is processed to produce transcripts and marketing-relevant insights. Where feasible, processing occurs on-device or is minimized; raw audio is retained only as long as needed for transcription and quality assurance, then deleted per Section 8.
- **Third-party voices.** In meetings, the Device may capture the voices of people around you. **You are responsible for obtaining any consent required from other participants before recording them**, in accordance with applicable law and basic courtesy. We provide in-product prompts and indicator lights to support this.
- **Handshake feature.** If you enable Handshake (proximity-based founder matching), the Device shares a limited profile (name, company, stated intent) with nearby mrk18 users **only after both parties opt in to a specific introduction**. Location/proximity signals used for Handshake are ephemeral and not used to build a movement history.
- **Sensors.** Battery, connectivity, and diagnostic data are collected to keep the Device functioning and improve reliability.

### 2.4 Information collected automatically
- **Usage data:** features used, queries asked (text of prompts), session times, in-app actions.
- **Device/technical data:** IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, crash logs.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** see Section 11.

### 2.5 Information we do NOT intentionally collect
We do not seek, and ask that you do not submit: government IDs, financial account passwords, health data, or data about children. If such data appears incidentally in audio or connected accounts, we do not use it for any purpose and delete it where identified.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

1. **Provide the core service** — analyze your marketing data, generate advice, verdicts, content, scripts, competitor and USP analysis, and (on your instruction) execute campaigns.
2. **Personalize your experience** — mrk18 maintains a **per-account context memory** so that answer #50 is sharper than answer #1. This memory is scoped to your account.
3. **Operate accounts and billing** — authentication, subscription management, invoicing, plan limits.
4. **Communicate with you** — service updates, onboarding, security alerts, and (with your consent) product news. You can opt out of marketing emails at any time.
5. **Improve the Services** — aggregate, de-identified analytics on feature usage and model quality.
6. **Safety, security, and legal compliance** — fraud prevention, abuse detection, enforcing our Terms, complying with law and lawful requests.

### 3.1 AI processing — what we do and don't do
- Your prompts, connected marketing data, and voice transcripts are processed by our AI systems (including fine-tuned models and third-party inference infrastructure) to generate responses.
- **Your data is used to build your account's context and memory. It is not used to train our global models in a way that could reproduce your confidential business information for other customers.**
- If we ever offer an option to contribute data to model improvement, it will be **opt-in**, clearly labeled, and revocable.
- AI outputs are generated content. See our Terms of Service for disclaimers regarding reliance on AI advice.

## 5. How We Share Information

We do **not** sell your personal data. We share information only with:

1. **Service providers (Data Processors):** cloud hosting, GPU/inference providers, transcription services, payment processors, email/communication tools, and analytics providers — each bound by contracts limiting use of your data to providing services to us.

3. **Handshake counterparties:** only the limited profile described in Section 2.3, and only after mutual opt-in.
4. **Legal and safety:** courts, regulators, or law enforcement where required by applicable law, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of mrk18, our users, or the public.
5. **Business transfers:** in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of the transaction, subject to this Policy's protections. We will notify you of any such change.

We may share **aggregated or de-identified information** (e.g., "X% of founders overspend on channel Y") that cannot reasonably identify you.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

- **Account data:** retained while your account is active.
- **Business context and memory:** retained while your account is active, so advice keeps improving; deleted on account deletion.
- **Raw audio:** retained only for the period needed for transcription and quality checks — no longer than 30 days — then deleted. Transcripts persist as part of your account context unless you delete them.
- **Connected marketing data:** refreshed on sync; historical snapshots retained to power trend analysis; deleted on disconnection or account deletion.
- **Payment and tax records:** retained as required by Indian law (typically 7–8 years under tax and company law).
- **Backups:** deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for up to 90 days before being purged.

When you delete your account, we delete or irreversibly de-identify your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law. To request deletion, email **mrk18ai@gmail.com** with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Disconnecting a Meta or Google account from within mrk18 revokes our access tokens and stops further data collection from that account immediately.
