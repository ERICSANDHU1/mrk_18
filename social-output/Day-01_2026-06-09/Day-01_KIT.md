## 3 · ERIC — LinkedIn (THE WORKSHOP)

Wrote 50 tests last night. All 50 passed. Green the whole way down.

And I still sat there at 1am thinking: but is this actually right?

The tests check that the code does what I told it to do. They don't check that what I told it to do is the right thing for a founder staring at their marketing at 11pm.

So I deleted a chunk and started a harder check — not "does it run" but "would this change what she does tomorrow morning".

Green isn't the same as right. Took me 50 passing tests to feel that one in my gut.

How do you tell the difference in your own work?

### Why these (signal-backed)
- **Scene-first hooks, no question-openers** — curiosity/personal-story hooks out-perform question hooks; the first ~140 chars are the whole game on mobile.
- **Failure/doubt over wins** — Eric's "is this right?" and Pritam's "silence" beat polished wins for trust + engagement.
- **No links** — external links (and the first-comment link trick) suppress reach in 2026; Day 1 drives comments/DMs, not clicks.
- **Saveable + debate-worthy** — each ends on a genuine one-line question to pull 15+-word comments (which now carry ~2.5x weight).
- **Registers:** Pritam = Warikoo-style earned observation; Eric = dry builder candor; company = Vembu-style quiet conviction + build-from-India. Zero agency/competitor mentions — the bitter truth points at the data, not at anyone.
