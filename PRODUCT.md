# Product

## Register

product

*(The landing page, film, and auth threshold run in the brand register per task; the dashboard — Chat / Comrk / Chief — is the product default.)*

## Users

Founders everywhere (global targeting as of 2026-07-02; the product is India-born but not India-scoped) — mostly early-stage D2C and SaaS, not marketers by training, time-poor, spending their own money on ads. They arrive skeptical of agencies and of AI fluff. Context: late nights, real money on the line, decisions made between operational fires.

## Product Purpose

mrk18 is an AI CMO. It reads the founder's real numbers, flags what's leaking money, and hands them the next move in plain language. Runs produce strategy and platform-native content behind explicit approval gates (Gate 1 strategy, Gate 2 content) — the founder approves everything before it ships. Success = a founder trusts the verdicts enough to act on them weekly.

A hardware companion is launching alongside: **mrk — the CMO in your pocket**, a pocket device (glossy puck, teased on /mrk and the auth panel with the Radar icon + "Launching soon"). Treat it as a first-class brand element.

## Brand Personality

Candid, warm, sharp. The "bitter-truth CMO": says the uncomfortable thing plainly, never flatters, never invents numbers. Warm founder-to-founder tone, zero corporate jargon, globally legible (no region-locked copy). The product speaks in first person ("your CMO") and that voice is allowed to appear in the UI at key moments.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-SaaS slop: purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism-by-default, floating 3D blobs. (Founder explicitly rejected liquid-glass and WebGL node-graph directions for the dashboard.)
- Cool palettes (blue/violet) — clash with the warm brand; rejected.
- Invented metrics or fake testimonials anywhere. The product's whole promise is zero-hallucination truth; marketing surfaces must practice it.
- Orange-everywhere chrome. Accent is spent deliberately; night mode deliberately quiets it.

## Design Principles

1. **Truth over flattery** — copy states real capability; no invented numbers, stars, or logos.
2. **Warm restraint** — warm sand / terracotta / ink palette; in the app, color = status, never ornament.
3. **The founder approves everything** — UI must make review and approval effortless; pending decisions are always the loudest thing on screen.
4. **Plain language** — a non-marketer must understand every label and verdict on first read.
5. **Let the CMO speak** — the product's voice (bitter-truth, first person) appears at thresholds and moments of guidance; that voice is the brand's imagery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (≥4.5:1 body text). `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on every animation (established pattern across the landing film and the app). Keyboard focus visible (molten outline). English UI today; plain-language copy doubles as low-English-fluency accommodation.
