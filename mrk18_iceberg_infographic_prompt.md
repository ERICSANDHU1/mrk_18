# mrk18 — "AI Iceberg" Infographic: Production Prompt + LinkedIn Caption

Design is locked to the reference (same style, layout, palette, typography). Two content options below — **A** keeps the original content unchanged; **B** is an optional mrk18-native swap using the *identical* design.

> **Text-rendering tip:** diffusion image models often garble small text. For crisp, accurate labels use **GPT Image 2, Ideogram v3, or Google Imagen**, or hand this brief to a designer in **Figma / Canva** (recommended for pixel-perfect text). The prompt works as both an image-gen prompt and a design brief.

## MASTER PROMPT — Option A (faithful replica, content unchanged)

```
A clean, modern corporate SaaS infographic on a soft vertical blue gradient background
(pale sky-blue at the top fading to deep navy at the bottom), enclosed in a rounded-rectangle
card with a thin light-grey border.

Centerpiece: a low-poly, geometric faceted ICEBERG. Its small white/pale tip rises above a
horizontal DASHED white "waterline" near the top third; the massive dark navy-blue faceted
body extends far below the line. Above the waterline on the LEFT, a small tracked-out uppercase
label "APPROVED"; below the waterline on the RIGHT, a tracked-out uppercase label "NOT APPROVED".

A white rounded callout box connects to the tip with a thin leader line:
  Title (bold): "Approved AI"
  Subtitle: "Reviewed, contracted, signed off."

FOUR white rounded callout cards, each with a small BLUE circular number badge (white numeral)
and a thin leader line pointing to the submerged iceberg:
  1 (upper-left)  "AI features" — "AI capabilities inside applications already in use."
                   italic grey: "e.g. Microsoft 365 Copilot, Notion AI, Salesforce Einstein."
  2 (upper-right) "AI via APIs" — "AI models used directly through APIs or developer tools."
                   italic grey: "e.g. OpenAI API, Anthropic Console, Google AI Studio."
  3 (mid-left)    "AI browser extensions" — "AI features inside browser extensions."
                   italic grey: "e.g. Grammarly, Otter, Fireflies."
  4 (lower-right) "Personal AI accounts" — "AI tools used through individual, non-governed
                   employee accounts." italic grey: "e.g. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini."

Bottom-center, two lines of centered text:
  Regular weight: "You can't govern what you can't see."
  Bold:           "AI governance starts with a complete inventory."

STYLE: minimalist enterprise/SaaS design, Inter / Helvetica sans-serif, generous white space,
subtle soft drop-shadows on the white cards, flat vector look, calm and professional.
Palette STRICTLY blues, navy, white, and grey. Square or portrait aspect ratio, high resolution.
```
