## LinkedIn (mrk18 page)

Every founder has a marketing dashboard.

Almost none of them can answer the one question that actually matters from it: what should I do tomorrow?

That's not a founder problem. Dashboards were built for analysts — people whose job is to stare at charts and interpret them. Founders don't have that job. They're running marketing between standups, hiring, and a dozen fires, and they need a decision, not a data exhibit.

A dashboard tells you what happened. It shows impressions, clicks, open rates — and leaves the hard part (so what?) entirely to you.

We think the next layer is different. An AI CMO reads the same numbers, tells you the bitter truth about what's working versus quietly leaking money, and hands you the next move in plain language.

Not another chart. A decision you can act on by Monday.

We're building it for founders who run marketing without a marketing team.

If your dashboard could say one honest sentence to you each Monday morning, what would you want it to say?
